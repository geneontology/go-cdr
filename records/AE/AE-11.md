# AE-11. Keep extensions to what a single statement can hold. Causal, chained or multi-activity knowledge goes in a GO-CAM.

## Decision

Keep extensions to what a single statement can hold. Causal, chained or multi-activity knowledge goes in a GO-CAM.

## Context

GO is separating standard annotations from GO-CAMs. A standard annotation is one GAF or GPAD line, one evidence, independent or dependent extensions and no nesting. Chained relations will be archived, and groups are encouraged to rebuild that knowledge as true GO-CAMs ([Drive: GO-CAMs and standard annotations in Noctua (Fall 2026 GOC)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1CY1Py4e9jTSXDn_mircd-C3aVLHqNJ2x3BvH90EKFj8); [Drive: Evidence unwinding meeting (2026-01-20)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1IsQ9cY3eJCkmpU6VAlJiinrm_Bztt_VZcKv98QPjsX8)). The 2022 relation review already proposed removing causal relations from extensions.

**Rule detail.**

- "A regulates B's activity": a GO-CAM edge, not `directly_positively_regulates` in column 16 of A's binding annotation. The 2019 `activated_by` reannotation used that pattern ([wiki: Annotation Conf. Call 2019-11-19](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotation_Conf._Call_2019-11-19)). Read rows that already carry it as legacy GO-CAM fragments.
- "Regulates transcription of gene G in cell type C": one standard annotation with `has_input`(G),`occurs_in`(C) is fine, because that is a single statement.
- When a standard-annotation extension and a GO-CAM say different things about the same gene, the GO-CAM is the more specific model. Check whether the extension is a stale import ([gocam-modeling.md](../CAM/README.md)).

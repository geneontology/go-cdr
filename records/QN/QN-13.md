# QN-13. Do not make NOT annotations to binding terms.

## Decision

Do not make NOT annotations to binding terms.

## Context

gorule-0000002 filters NOT annotations to GO:0005515 protein binding, and the 2024 rules update extended the filter to GO:0005488 binding ([Drive: GO rules (2024-05 GOC meeting)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1CmnWtTKkx8Qo3e7XNEFfafZYTGTrJq7DcqRVBdB7lzM)). Failure to bind one partner in one assay is not a property worth negating, and the bare binding terms are uninformative even when positive. A specific binding term with a functional consequence can still be negated when a reader would expect it. Example: NOT GO:0005544 calcium-dependent phospholipid binding for a synaptotagmin that has lost its calcium-binding residues.

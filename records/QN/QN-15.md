# QN-15. Use NOT to stop propagation of a lost activity, and know how PAINT treats NOT.

## Decision

Use NOT to stop propagation of a lost activity, and know how PAINT treats NOT.

## Context

A REMOVE in one review cleans one row, and the next IEA or IBA run reasserts it. A NOT filed upstream, together with an IKR or IRD loss on the PAINT node, stops the reassertion ([TOP_NOTS](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/TOP_NOTS.html)). Reviewers need the PAINT logic to read the IBA rows they meet.

**PAINT logic, current and historical.**

- *2014 rules* ([wiki: 11 MAR 2014 PAINT Conference Call](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/11_MAR_2014_PAINT_Conference_Call)): a NOT propagates only to the term annotated, never to parents. When positive and NOT experimental annotations meet at the same level, or a positive on a specific term meets a NOT on a general one, the NOT is ignored and the positive propagates. A positive general term still propagates into a clade where a specific child is blocked. Experimental NOTs inside a clade did not stop propagation automatically, but conflicting leaves were silently skipped.
- *2021 change* ([wiki: 31 Aug 2021 PAINT Conference Call](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/31_Aug_2021_PAINT_Conference_Call)): the PAINT tool propagates IBA to leaves that carry a conflicting experimental NOT and highlights the conflict, and the curator must block it with IKR or IRD. The call recorded concerns about curation load and agreed to keep the older script-based release until conflicts could be reviewed. Which behaviour produces a given release has to be checked against the PAINT README.
- *Loss codes.* Use IKR (ECO:0000320) when the residues that define the activity are lost, and IRD (ECO:0000321 rapid divergence from ancestral sequence evidence used in manual assertion) for divergence without a residue-level explanation ([go-annotation#2109](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2109)). An IRD produces no leaf row, so the repo tool `gene_paint_losses` lists the losses on a gene's lineage for review.
- *Conflict sources* ([Drive: Review NOT annotation in PAINT (2022)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Db0k2fuFzWYNoiHVGamYlJuhPm3bAku0)): among paralogues after a duplication, conflicts usually mean rapid divergence or loss of active sites, and PAINT should place a loss. In mammals they usually mean an isoform-based NOT ([QN-10](QN-10.md)). Within one gene, they should be resolved at literature curation.

**Review procedure.** When an IBA contradicts a curated NOT on the same gene, do not ACCEPT the IBA by deference ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/main/CLAUDE.md), IBA section). Check whether the NOT is isoform-scoped. If it is not, and the NOT is sound, REMOVE the IBA row and record the needed IKR or IRD as the PAINT fix (`paint_action: ADD_IKR` or `ADD_IRD` in a family review). The `Hyal2` case in [NAKED_MOLE_RAT](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/NAKED_MOLE_RAT.html), a virus-receptor term propagated from a donor set that contains a curated NOT for the same term, is the pattern.

**Proposing a new NOT (repo).** A review cannot file a NOT upstream directly. When a REMOVE rests on demonstrated absence (pseudoenzyme, catalytic residues lost, assay-negative), say so in `reason` with the evidence. The [TOP_NOTS](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/TOP_NOTS.html) scan collects such rows as candidates for submission.

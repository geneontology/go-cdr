# QN-14. A NOT needs a positive annotation in the same aspect, the ND root if nothing else is known. A NOT row does not make an ND row stale.

## Decision

A NOT needs a positive annotation in the same aspect, the ND root if nothing else is known. A NOT row does not make an ND row stale.

## Context

A NOT says what the product does not do and nothing about what it does. The ND wiki requires a positive annotation alongside every NOT, using the root term with ND when nothing else is known ([wiki: ND evidence code](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/No_biological_Data_available_%28ND%29_evidence_code)). The repo's ND policy ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/main/CLAUDE.md); [NO_DATA](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/NO_DATA.html)) asks whether literature on the gene *identifies a function* in the aspect. A NOT identifies an absence, so it does not make a co-existing ND stale, any more than a bare protein-binding IPI does.

## Application

**Example.** A pseudokinase with `NOT enables` GO:0004672 (IKR) and no known activity should also carry GO:0003674 molecular_function (ND). Accept the ND row if the literature search shows no positive function.

# QN-11. A NOT to a molecular function is the strongest use of the qualifier. A NOT to a process needs evidence that the gene product was unable to do the process's work.

## Decision

A NOT to a molecular function is the strongest use of the qualifier. A NOT to a process needs evidence that the gene product was unable to do the process's work.

## Context

The repo audit of all 10,622 GOA NOT rows (2026-10-01) found the useful end of NOT concentrated in MF: 4,664 MF rows, 3,405 of them catalytic, mostly IBA, IDA and IKR. These are pseudoenzymes and diverged paralogues. Process NOTs drift toward phenotype and expression nulls. UniProt CAUTION notes follow the same split: 19.5% of reviewed proteins with an MF NOT carry a loss-of-activity CAUTION, against 1.9% of proteins whose NOTs are only to processes or locations ([NOT_ANNOTATION_USAGE](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/NOT_ANNOTATION_USAGE.html)).

**Rule detail.** A process NOT is acceptable when it is really an activity negative stated as a process. CRY2 NOT photoreactive repair is one. So is "NOT response to X" for a receptor shown not to bind and transduce X. "NOT response to X" for an abundance or expression null is not acceptable.

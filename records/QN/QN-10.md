# QN-10. A positive and a NOT annotation to the same term may coexist only when the literature genuinely conflicts or the claims concern different gene products (isoforms). The reviewer must reconcile each pair in writing and narrow the NOT where the conflict is only apparent.

## Decision

A positive and a NOT annotation to the same term may coexist only when the literature genuinely conflicts or the claims concern different gene products (isoforms). The reviewer must reconcile each pair in writing and narrow the NOT where the conflict is only apparent.

## Context

GO allows the pair. It has been confirmed on the tracker ([go-annotation#1761](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1761)) and in the wiki ("Both positive and NOT statements can be used in cases where there is conflicting experimental findings"). But as a pair of logical statements they cannot both be true of the same entity, so each pair is either a real dispute, a scope mismatch or an isoform difference.

**Resolution procedure.**

1. *Isoform.* If one row was made on an isoform (`isoform: P19544-1`), record the isoform on both. The pair is then consistent. The 2022 PAINT review found that many mammalian NOT-versus-IBA conflicts came from NOTs made on splice variants ([Drive: Review NOT annotation in PAINT (2022)](https://drive.google.com/open?id=1Db0k2fuFzWYNoiHVGamYlJuhPm3bAku0)).
2. *Scope.* If the NOT excludes a general form of the activity and the positive shows a specific form, the NOT is too broad. Move it to the descendant the evidence excludes ([QN-9](QN-9.md)). If no descendant expresses the distinction, keep both, explain the distinction in each `reason`, and record an ontology gap.
3. *Real conflict.* Decide which result is better supported (methods, replication, later papers). Keep that row. REMOVE the other only if the later evidence refutes it; otherwise mark it UNDECIDED. Do not resolve the conflict by deleting the NOT simply because a positive exists.

## Application

**Worked example (COQ8B).** `genes/human/COQ8B` has `enables` GO:0004672 protein kinase activity (IDA, PMID:38425362: COQ3 is phosphorylated by reconstructed ancestral COQ8B in vitro) and `NOT enables` GO:0004672 (IDA, PMID:27499294: no canonical kinase activity in trans). The review keeps both and reconciles them in prose: the NOT concerns general activity in trans, the positive one substrate. GO cannot express the distinction. NOT with a `has_input` extension is disallowed ([QN-12](QN-12.md)), and no GO term describes "kinase activity on general substrates in trans". This is an expressivity gap, not a curation error.

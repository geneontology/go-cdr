# QN-16. Review an existing NOT row as a claim of absence: ACCEPT, MODIFY (narrow the term or fix the isoform), REMOVE or UNDECIDED. Do not mark it non-core, over-annotated or too general.

## Decision

Review an existing NOT row as a claim of absence: ACCEPT, MODIFY (narrow the term or fix the isoform), REMOVE or UNDECIDED. Do not mark it non-core, over-annotated or too general.

## Context

Core and non-core describe functions, and a NOT is not a function. "Over-annotated" and "too general" have no clear meaning for a negation. A NOT on a general term is the *strongest* negation, so a broad NOT is an error of scope that MODIFY should fix. The rule mirrors the repo's ND rule. The repo's reviews have been deferential so far: of 270 negated rows in 174 reviews, 223 were ACCEPTed, 20 UNDECIDED, 16 KEEP_AS_NON_CORE and 8 REMOVEd ([NOT_ANNOTATION_USAGE](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/NOT_ANNOTATION_USAGE.html)).

**Rule detail.**

- ACCEPT when [QN-8](QN-8.md) is met and the term is at the right level.
- MODIFY to a descendant when the NOT is too high ([QN-9](QN-9.md)), or to add the isoform.
- REMOVE when the evidence is a negative or inconclusive result, an expression or abundance null, or a non-expected absence.
- UNDECIDED when the full text cannot be checked and the abstract does not settle it. The repo's rule against overruling curators from abstracts applies to NOTs too.

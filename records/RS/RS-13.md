# RS-13. Do not use MF regulator terms when the regulatory activity is unknown, and do not give a regulator the activity it regulates

## Decision

**Statement.** If a gene product is known to regulate a process but its molecular activity is unknown, annotate the BP regulation term and leave MF unannotated. GO:0098772 molecular function regulator activity is do-not-annotate. Conversely, when a gene product regulates an enzyme or receptor, annotate it with the regulator activity (enzyme activator, inhibitor, ligand), not the activity of its target.

## Context

[go-annotation#2827](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2827): "We don't recommend using a MF if we don't know what the MF is. You can use BP 'regulation of x pathway'". The pilot function-requirement rules in this repository found that regulators annotated with the activity they regulate are a recurring class among flagged experimental `enables` rows ([FUNCTION_REQUIREMENT_RULES](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/FUNCTION_REQUIREMENT_RULES.html)). The GTPase case is the standard example: a GEF is not a GTPase activator ([go-annotation#1166](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1166)).

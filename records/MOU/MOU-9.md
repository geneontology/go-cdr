# MOU-9. Hand over annotation responsibility when the group can no longer maintain it

## Decision

A group that can no longer maintain its annotations transfers them to another group. If no suitable group is available, GO Central takes them over.

## Context

This keeps every annotation in GO maintained by someone. GO Central also takes over the annotations of a group that does not respond after repeated contact.

# MOU-7. Submit annotations through a supported route

## Decision

A group submits annotations by curating in, or submitting through, Protein2GO or Noctua, or by providing a zipped GAF 2.2 file at a public URL described in the go-site dataset metadata.

## Context

Supported routes let GO Central validate, integrate and credit the annotations.

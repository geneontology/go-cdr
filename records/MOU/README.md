# Memorandum of understanding for contributing groups

The GO Consortium (GOC) is a collaboration of groups that contribute GO annotations in
their areas of expertise. The memorandum of understanding (MOU) sets out what GO Central
and the contributing groups each undertake. Each record in this chapter is one obligation
of a contributing group; a group signs off on each (or on the whole chapter) in its file
under `groups/`.

## Who is involved

- **GO Central** coordinates the GO project and stewards the knowledge base. It develops
  the ontology and the Noctua curation tool, oversees the annotation guidelines, and
  distributes and archives the data.
- **GOA**, the GO Annotation team at EBI, integrates and validates contributed data,
  including the IEA pipelines.
- A **contributing group** is any person or team outside GO Central that creates or
  maintains GO annotations or ontology content used by the GOC.

## What GO Central undertakes

These are not signed off by groups; they are the other half of the agreement.

- Integrate, validate and release the knowledge base roughly monthly at geneontology.org,
  archived at Zenodo.
- Define the technical standards for exchanging annotations, GO-CAMs and ontology
  assertions, the automated checks (GORULES), and the APIs that implement them.
- Publish the curation guidelines, best practices and rubrics (the other chapters here).
- Develop the ontology, adding the terms groups need.
- Oversee QA/QC through the go-annotation tracker.
- Maintain the phylogenetic annotations (IBA, IBD) and add the current IBA set to every
  group's annotations at each release.

GOA integrates data from all sources, including the IBA and IEA pipelines, runs quality
control, and shares error reports with each group.

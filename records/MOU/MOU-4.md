# MOU-4. Respond promptly to GOC requests about the group's annotations

## Decision

A group responds promptly to requests about its annotations, whether they come through GitHub, the annotation calls, the error reports or other channels.

## Context

What counts as prompt depends on the circumstances. Where its own responsibilities require it, GO Central may repair or work around a group's data.

# MOU-10. Onboard and offboard members with the GOC procedures

## Decision

New members follow the GOC onboarding procedure, learn the annotation guidelines and have an experienced GOC member to turn to; departing members follow the offboarding procedure so their work carries on.

## Context

Turnover is normal; the procedures keep it from stranding annotations or access.

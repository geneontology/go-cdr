# MOU-2. Annotate according to the published guidelines and keep annotations current

## Decision

A group annotates according to the published GOC guidelines and documentation, and updates its annotations as the ontology and annotation practice change.

## Context

Consistency across groups is what makes GO usable as one resource. The guideline chapters in this repository are a draft of what "the published guidelines" will mean once accepted; a group's sign-off on a guideline chapter records whether it follows it.

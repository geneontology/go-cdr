# MOU-1. Contribute and maintain annotations within a declared scope

## Decision

A group contributes GO annotations and keeps maintaining them, within a declared scope: all annotations for one or more species, or for a project or area of biology.

## Context

A declared scope tells users and GO Central who answers for which annotations, and lets GO Central find gaps and overlaps between groups.

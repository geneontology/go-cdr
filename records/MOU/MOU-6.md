# MOU-6. Stay informed about consortium decisions

## Decision

For a group that is actively curating, at least one member attends the GO monthly update calls and the twice-yearly consortium meetings, or at least reads their minutes.

## Context

Changes to the ontology, guidelines and pipelines are announced and discussed there.

# MOU-11. Release contributions under CC BY 4.0

## Decision

By contributing to the GOC, a group agrees that its contributions are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license, with copyright held by the Gene Ontology Consortium.

## Context

Every annotation records the group that made it, so each group keeps clear credit. Citation follows the GO citation policy.

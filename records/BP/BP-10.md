# BP-10. Cell death: choose the phase, keep regulation for specific effects, and do not equate death with apoptosis

## Decision

**Statement.** For apoptosis, annotate the phase when the evidence identifies it: extrinsic (GO:0097191) or intrinsic (GO:0097193) apoptotic signalling, with GO:0097190 apoptotic signaling pathway used downstream of the BID crosstalk point or when the initiating signal is unknown, and GO:0097194 execution phase of apoptosis. Annotate GO:0006915 apoptotic process directly only when the phase cannot be told. Use regulation terms (GO:0043065, GO:0043066 and children) only when the gene product specifically modulates the process. A side effect of disrupting a major pathway does not qualify. A viability defect alone does not show apoptosis.

## Context

These are the Apoptosis Curation Manual's central rules ([wiki: Apoptosis Curation Manual](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Apoptosis_Curation_Manual)). The manual also records the obsoletion of cell death terms that "did not represent normal, physiological processes", and states the current practice that the mechanism of death must be known before annotating. [go-annotation#1056](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1056) rehoused "induction of apoptosis" annotations on the same principle. This repository's apoptosis work found that readout-based rows are usually true but non-core. Of 72 mined readout rows, 35 were kept as non-core and 27 accepted ([APOPTOSIS assay readouts](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/APOPTOSIS/ASSAY_READOUT_OVERANNOTATION.html)).

## Application

**Worked examples.**

- Human CASP3 rows on GO:0006915 can usually move to GO:0097194 execution phase of apoptosis, because the caspase's role in that phase is direct ([APOPTOSIS](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/APOPTOSIS.html), curation heuristics).
- Mouse Pten, GO:0006915 from genetic interaction data: the apoptosis outcome lies downstream of PTEN/PI3K/AKT signalling, so `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` ([APOPTOSIS assay readouts](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/APOPTOSIS/ASSAY_READOUT_OVERANNOTATION.html)).
- Restraint is regulation. BCL2, XIAP and CFLAR are direct regulators of apoptosis even though their function is to prevent it ([APOPTOSIS](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/APOPTOSIS.html)).
- For cell type, the manual's advice still holds: keep GO:0006915 and add the cell type (for example a neutrophil, CL:0000775) as an extension, rather than moving to GO:0001781 neutrophil apoptotic process and stranding ISS transfers to species that may lack the cell type ([wiki: Apoptosis Curation Manual](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Apoptosis_Curation_Manual)).

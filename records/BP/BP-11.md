# BP-11. Do not request, add or split precomposed "X involved in Y" terms; decompose them to X and record where Y went

## Decision

**Statement.** Do not request new "X involved in Y" terms, and do not use them for new annotations when the X term exists. When reviewing a row on such a term:

## Context

1. Read the logical definition. If it has no `part_of`, the "involved in" is either entailed by X (regulation of MOMP) or absent (grouping classes). Keep the term.
2. Otherwise decide whether X alone still says what the gene does. If it does, `MODIFY` to X, and record in `review.decomposed_into` where Y went: a GO-CAM or module, the gene's core functions, or `context_dropped: true`.
3. Never split the row into two unrelated annotations (`involved_in` X plus `involved_in` Y). Never re-encode Y as an extension `part_of(Y)` on X, which restates the retired class. Extensions that add cell type or location (`occurs_in`) are not decomposition and remain fine.
4. Justify each half separately. A decomposition is a review, not a reformat.

**Rationale.** GO has obsoleted more than a thousand precomposed terms, most with the comment that the term "should be captured as a GO-CAM model". Of 784 live terms with this label pattern, many remain in use ([INVOLVED_IN_PRECOMPOSITION](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/INVOLVED_IN_PRECOMPOSITION.html)). `replaced_by` keeps one half automatically. `consider` lists both halves, but it is curator advice, so check each half against the evidence ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/main/CLAUDE.md)). Some terms are the right grain despite the label. GO:0106074 aminoacyl-tRNA metabolism involved in translational fidelity names editing, which has no other GO term. GO:0006271 DNA strand elongation involved in DNA replication names a stage ([INVOLVED_IN_PRECOMPOSITION](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/INVOLVED_IN_PRECOMPOSITION.html), round 5).

## Application

**Worked examples.**

- Human HSPA8, GO:0061740 protein targeting to lysosome involved in chaperone-mediated autophagy: MODIFY to GO:0006622 protein targeting to lysosome. Y (GO:0061684 chaperone-mediated autophagy) is a mechanism, so it goes to the chaperone-mediated autophagy module.
- *C. elegans* engulfment genes ced-1, ced-6 and ced-7, GO:1904747 positive regulation of apoptotic process involved in development: MODIFY to GO:0043065 positive regulation of apoptotic process with `context_dropped: true`. The developmental context is where the pro-death role was measured, not a mechanism. The gene's own GOA rows on the same claim move with the replacements, so that no gene proposes a term while keeping its child ([INVOLVED_IN_PRECOMPOSITION](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/INVOLVED_IN_PRECOMPOSITION.html), round 6).

# BP-14. In review, separate core processes from real but peripheral ones, and reserve REMOVE for contradicted claims

## Decision

**Statement.** A process is core when the gene product's core activity is a step of it. A process is non-core when the claim is true but peripheral: a downstream outcome, a tissue context, a pleiotropic consequence. Map the cases to actions:

## Context

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Activity is a step of the process | `ACCEPT` |
| Real, reproducible effect, distal to the activity | `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE`, with the proximal defect named in `reason` |
| Effect real but the term overstates it (uselessly broad, or an expression correlate read as a role) | `MARK_AS_OVER_ANNOTATED` |
| Same claim, better term available from this row's evidence | `MODIFY` |
| Contradicted (wrong gene or paralog, opposite sign, refuted mechanism, demonstrably wrong mapping) | `REMOVE` |
| Evidence inaccessible or unadjudicated | `UNDECIDED` |

**Rationale.** The GOC wiki says older phenotype-based annotations "should be reviewed and removed" when new knowledge shows they do not reflect the gene's MF–BP relation ([wiki: Annotating from phenotypes](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotating_from_phenotypes)). It also acknowledges that groups differ, and that some keep downstream annotations as a record ([wiki: Annotating downstream processes](https://wiki.geneontology.org/index.php/Annotating_downstream_processes)). In a review we cannot see the curator's full text ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/main/CLAUDE.md), "Do not overrule curators from incomplete evidence"). Demotion preserves the data and still removes the row from the core picture. Rows that share a term must receive consistent actions, and REMOVE is never a way to deduplicate ([docs/action_consistency.md](https://github.com/ai4curation/ai-gene-review/blob/main/docs/action_consistency.md)).

## Application

**Worked examples.** Mouse Agtr1a, `drinking behavior`: direct central-angiotensin experiments assign the behaviour to the paralog AT1B, so the claim is contradicted and `REMOVE` is correct. Mouse Tuba1a, `locomotory behavior`: distal, so `KEEP_AS_NON_CORE` ([BEHAVIOR](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/BEHAVIOR.html)).

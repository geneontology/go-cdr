# BP-15. Do not propagate developmental, behavioural or stimulus-response terms without a conserved mechanism, and check that the pathway can exist in the target taxon

## Decision

**Statement.** Organism-level process terms (development, behaviour, physiology) and "response to" terms should not be transferred by orthology, ISS or IBA unless the mechanism that places the gene in the process is conserved and the process exists in the target lineage. A pathway term whose definition requires a component the target genome lacks is unsatisfiable there.

## Context

[go-annotation#1344](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1344) argued against Ensembl Compara propagation of developmental and response-to-substance terms and proposed a not-for-propagation flag. [go-annotation#1364](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1364) concluded that downstream processes regulated by signalling molecules should not be propagated unless broadly conserved. PAINT does not normally propagate "response to" terms ([go-annotation#2171](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2171); [go-annotation#2250](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/2250)). Taxon constraints enforce part of this (GORULE:0000013; [Drive: Taxon constraints for signaling pathways, Padua 2023](https://drive.google.com/open?id=117j8ysvt6FpVR0KCeFgrRDjUjm3jVXm8-nNReVU-hEg)).

## Application

**Worked example.** *Dictyostelium* STATa/c carried GO:0007259 cell surface receptor signaling pathway via JAK-STAT, but *Dictyostelium* has no JAK. The rows were rescoped to the JAK-independent parent GO:0097696 cell surface receptor signaling pathway via STAT ([PATHWAY_SATISFIABILITY taxon-absent-component detector](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/PATHWAY_SATISFIABILITY/taxon_absent_component/README.html)). See [phylogenetic-propagation.md](../PH/README.md) for the IBA side.

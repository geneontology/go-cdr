# BP-12. Annotate the step term when the evidence reaches it; do not demand BP specificity that only restates the MF

## Decision

**Statement.** If the evidence identifies the step (for example a named cascade or a phase), annotate the step term. Its pathway ancestors follow by inference, so do not add them separately from the same evidence. Annotate a pathway-level term directly only when no step term fits or the evidence stops at the pathway, for example a genetic requirement with no step assignment, which takes an upstream relation under BP-4. Do not push toward a BP child whose only added specificity repeats a fact already asserted in MF.

## Context

Ontology inference propagates `involved_in` over `is_a` and `part_of`, so a step annotation already says the gene is in the pathway. The cross-aspect case is documented in [OVER_ANNOTATION_PATTERNS](https://ai4curation.io/ai-gene-review/pages/projects/OVER_ANNOTATION_PATTERNS.html), pattern 9. The amino-acid-specific tRNA aminoacylation BP terms (GO:0006419 alanyl-tRNA aminoacylation and its siblings) each pair one-to-one with an MF that already names the amino acid (GO:0004813 alanine-tRNA ligase activity). GO directs annotation to the MF plus the compartment-level process. The same caution applies to step terms under regulation. An assay that reads out one step does not show that the step is where regulation acts. A gene product that blocks a cargo's nuclear translocation is better annotated to GO:0042308 negative regulation of protein import into nucleus than to regulation of the translocation step, because regulating that step would imply the earlier steps, such as docking, proceed normally ([go-annotation#1011](https://github.com/geneontology/go-annotation/issues/1011); see [RS-2](../RS/RS-2.md)).

## Application

**Worked example.** Annotate MAP2K1 `involved_in` GO:0000165 MAPK cascade (a cassette, GO:0141124 intracellular signaling cassette). Annotate the receptor pathway it serves, such as GO:0007173 epidermal growth factor receptor signaling pathway, only when the evidence places it there (see [RS-7](../RS/RS-7.md)).
